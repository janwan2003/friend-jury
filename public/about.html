About Jurybox

Jurybox is a free “take it to the jury” link for group chats. One person writes the argument and their side, the other person adds theirs, and friends vote in secret until the deadline. Then the court rules: the vote split, the best one-line ruling, and a verdict card to share.

It is made for light, everyday arguments between friends, flatmates, couples and families: who was right about the restaurant, whether pineapple belongs on pizza, when the Christmas music may start. It is not for serious disputes, and either side can delete a case at any time.

No app, no sign-up and no ads. Privacy · Support
