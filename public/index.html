Questions

Do my friends need an app or an account?

No. Everyone opens the link in their browser, picks a side and types a nickname. That’s it.

Can people see how others voted?

Not until the verdict. During the trial everyone only sees how many jurors have voted and their nicknames. When the jury rules, every vote and ruling is shown.

When does the jury rule?

When the time you picked runs out (1, 2, 6 or 24 hours). Either side can also call the verdict early once at least three jurors have voted.

Can I delete a case?

Yes. Either side can delete the case at any time, and the other person can decline the invite. Cases are deleted automatically after 60 days.

Is it free?

Yes. No ads, no sign-up, nothing to install.
