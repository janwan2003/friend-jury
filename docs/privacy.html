Privacy

Last updated 21 November 2026.

Jurybox is built to need as little about you as possible. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking cookies.

What we store

  • The case: the question, each side’s name and argument, the deadline you picked.
  • Votes: each juror’s nickname, the side they picked and their optional one-line ruling, plus rulings’ upvotes.
  • A random browser key: your browser makes a random key and keeps it in local storage, so the site knows which case is yours and which vote is yours. We store only a one-way hash of it.
  • Abuse limits: your IP address is used to count requests per hour so nobody can flood the service. These counters are deleted within two hours.

Who can see it

Anyone with a case link can read the case. Until the verdict, nobody can see how anyone voted or what they wrote as a ruling; jurors’ nicknames are visible. If the sides chose to hide their names, the jury sees “Orange” and “Blue” until the verdict.

How long we keep it

Each case and its votes are deleted automatically 60 days after the case was opened. Either side can delete a case at any time with “Delete case”; the invited person can decline, which deletes it too.

Who runs the servers

Jurybox runs on Cloudflare Workers. Cloudflare processes requests to deliver the site. We don’t sell or share data with anyone.

Children

Jurybox is a general-audience game and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Contact

Questions or a removal request: open an issue at github.com/janwan2003/friend-jury.
